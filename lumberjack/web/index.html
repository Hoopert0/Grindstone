🤖 Autopilot

Trains every skill by itself - even a brand-new account in Lumbridge: combat basics first (Attack/Strength/Defence 10), a quick Prayer to 43, then always the lowest skill up to its next milestone (a better spot or every 10 levels) - it stays on a skill until it gets there - at the best spot for the level. It spawns tools, food and supplies and wears the best gear for its levels.
Stop after (below), and the Plan tab's options - updates between steps, resume after a restart, travel by teleport - apply too.

Firemaking

With Spawn missing tools on: spawns the best logs your level can light and burns them where you stand - nothing to set up.
Burn banked logs instead (with Spawn missing tools off)
Loop: bank → withdraw logs → walk to the burn spot → light them all → repeat until the bank runs out. Keeps your tinderbox (fetches one from the bank if needed). Uses Map, Bank at and Start from (Tools › Maps).

Fishing

Loop: fish until the backpack is nearly full → chop 1–2 logs → light a fire → use the raw fish on it → Cook All → drop the burnt (and cooked) fish → repeat. Carry only your tools: fishing net, tinderbox, axe. Uses Map, Fish at and Bank at (Tools › Maps).

  2. Put each fish in the backpack, pick its slot and name: learn raw, cooked (teaches Eat) and burnt.

Run options

Plan

Run tasks one after another, non-stop. Each step travels to its place, banks or drops what the last task left, then runs the task with its settings from the Settings tab. A step that gets stuck is recovered and retried. For the whole account in one click, pick 🤖 Autopilot under Controls instead.

Places

★ = built in (used by "auto"). Their spots are approximate: if one is off, stand at the right spot and save it under the same name - yours wins.

Auto login

Logs in by itself whenever the game shows its login screen - at start, after a crash restart or a disconnect. Saved only on this PC (configs\login.json, plain text), never uploaded or synced.

Skill check

Runs every skill once for 2 minutes at the right place for your level (about 40 minutes), then lists which ones earned XP and why any stopped. Copy the report and send it over when something isn't working. Also saved as logs\skill_check.txt.
